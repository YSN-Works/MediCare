What We Offer

Comprehensive Healthcare Solutions

General Health Checkup

A comprehensive health assessment that includes thorough physical examination, blood pressure monitoring, BMI calculation, and basic blood tests to evaluate your overall health status.

Common Symptoms Addressed Fatigue, fever, body aches, weakness, general discomfort
Duration 30-45 minutes
Fee Range Rs. 1,000 - 2,000

Pediatric Care

Specialized healthcare for infants, children, and adolescents including vaccinations, growth monitoring, nutritional counseling, and treatment of common childhood illnesses.

Common Symptoms Addressed Fever, cough, cold, allergies, growth concerns, vaccination schedules
Duration 20-30 minutes
Fee Range Rs. 800 - 1,500

Cardiology Services

Heart health monitoring including ECG, blood pressure management, cholesterol screening, and comprehensive cardiac risk assessment with personalized treatment plans.

Common Symptoms Addressed Chest pain, palpitations, shortness of breath, high blood pressure
Duration 45-60 minutes
Fee Range Rs. 2,000 - 3,500

Laboratory & Diagnostic Tests

In-house laboratory facility offering complete blood count (CBC), blood sugar tests, thyroid panel, lipid profile, liver & kidney function tests, urine analysis, and more.

Tests Available CBC, Blood Sugar, HbA1c, Thyroid, Lipid Profile, Liver & Kidney Function
Report Time Same day - 24 hours
Fee Range Rs. 500 - 5,000

Dermatology

Expert treatment for skin conditions including acne, eczema, psoriasis, fungal infections, allergic reactions, and cosmetic skin consultations.

Common Symptoms Addressed Skin rashes, acne, itching, pigmentation, hair loss, allergies
Duration 20-30 minutes
Fee Range Rs. 1,200 - 2,500

Diabetes Management

Comprehensive diabetes care including blood sugar monitoring, HbA1c tracking, insulin management, diet planning, and lifestyle modification guidance for Type 1 & Type 2 diabetes.

Common Symptoms Addressed High blood sugar, frequent urination, excessive thirst, weight changes
Duration 30-45 minutes
Fee Range Rs. 1,500 - 3,000
FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an appointment before coming?

While walk-ins are welcome, we highly recommend booking an appointment to avoid wait times. You can book via our website, WhatsApp, or by calling our reception.

What are the clinic timings?

Our clinic operates Monday to Saturday, 5:00 PM to 10:00 PM. We are closed on Sundays and public holidays.

What insurance companies do you accept?

We accept panels from State Life Insurance, Jubilee Insurance, and several other major insurance providers. Please contact reception for specific details about your insurance plan.

How long does a general consultation take?

A general consultation typically takes 20-30 minutes. However, complex cases or first-time visits may take up to 45 minutes to ensure thorough examination.

Do you offer home visit services?

Yes, we offer home visit services for elderly patients and emergency situations within Lahore. Additional charges apply. Please call or WhatsApp us for scheduling.

Are lab reports available online?

Yes, lab reports can be sent to your WhatsApp or email once ready. You can also collect hard copies from our reception during clinic hours.
